# Self-Review — Accepted Suggestions Log

This file is maintained manually by the operator. When you apply a
suggestion from a monthly self-review into `prompts/system.md` or a
strategy prompt, append a short entry below. The self-review job
reads the last ~8KB of this file and passes it to Opus as context
to prevent future suggestions from contradicting prior ones.

Format per entry (freeform; the example below is a starter, not a schema):

---

## [YYYY-MM-DD] Accepted from 2026-MM self-review

- **Target:** prompts/system.md — section "..."
- **Change:** 1-sentence summary of what was added/modified/removed
- **Supporting flag:** {strategy} / {dimension}
